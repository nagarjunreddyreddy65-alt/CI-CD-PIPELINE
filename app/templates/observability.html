Z-Score Threshold Formula ≥ 2.5 σ
Z = (x - μ) / σ
Where x is run telemetry, μ is the historical sample mean, and σ is the sample standard deviation. Outliers beyond 2.5σ represent the 99.4th percentile of resource surges.
